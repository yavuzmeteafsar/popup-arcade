# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 22, 2026_

Popup Arcade is a Chrome extension with small offline games. This policy explains what the extension does with your data.

## Summary

Popup Arcade does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

## What is stored

The extension saves your high scores and best times (for example, your best Hopproo score or your best Flagfield time on Easy) using `chrome.storage.local`. This data:

- stays on your device, inside your browser profile
- is never sent to the developer or to any third party
- contains no personal information
- is removed when you uninstall the extension

## What is not collected

- No personal or contact information
- No browsing history, page content or website data
- No location data
- No analytics, telemetry, cookies or tracking
- No advertising

## Network access

Popup Arcade makes no network requests. All games and assets are bundled with the extension and work offline.

## Permissions

The only permission requested is `storage`, used to save high scores locally.

## Children

The extension does not collect any data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published in this repository with a new date.
